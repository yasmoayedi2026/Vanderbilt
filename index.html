01 What predicts severe PGD at TGH · n = 263 with 37 events

Three signals, one model

Hover a row

Firth penalized ML

Jeffreys prior, profile-likelihood CIs, LRT p — appropriate at this event count; plain ML is biased away from the null here. Model p = 0.027 · McFadden R² 0.074. Red = p < 0.05.

THE CAVEAT — the DCD row is the least stable number in this table

All 12 DCD transplants are 2025–26, carrying 5 PGD events. Four of them lack an ischaemic time, so this model silently drops 3 of those 5 events — DCD here rests on 8 patients and 2 events. Refitting without ischaemic time keeps all 12: aOR 6.61 (1.86–22.79), p = 0.004 — same direction, far tighter.

In that fit LVAD bridge is 2.28 (1.00–5.07) p = 0.050 and pre-op ECMO 2.70 (1.02–6.75) p = 0.045.

Traferox × ischaemic-time interaction dropped: driven by complete separation (DCD + Traferox = 2/2 PGD, DCD without = 0/6 in the modelled subset), which rendered the main effect uninterpretable.
